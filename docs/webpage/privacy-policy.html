Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

EchoCraft is built to keep speech on your machine. We do not run an account service, we do not collect telemetry, and we do not sell data.

1. What we do not collect

MANTISWARE does not collect, store, or receive:

  • Voice recordings or microphone audio
  • Transcripts, notes, or meeting text
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers
  • An EchoCraft account — there isn’t one

2. What stays on your device

The desktop app stores the following locally:

  • Transcription history, notes, and settings in the app data directory
  • Speech and embedding models you download
  • Optional API keys, encrypted via the OS keychain (Keychain, DPAPI, or libsecret)

Uninstalling the app or deleting that data removes it. We never see it.

3. Optional cloud features

If you paste your own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, and similar), requests go from your computer to that provider. EchoCraft does not proxy, log, or resell those calls. Their privacy policies apply to the data you send them.

Screen context is off by default. When you enable it, a screenshot of the display under the cursor is attached to that one assistant request and is not written to disk or history.

4. Downloads and updates

  • App updates come from our Nextcloud share.
  • Speech and embedding models are downloaded from Hugging Face when you choose them.
  • This website is a static page. It does not set tracking cookies.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to record dictation and meetings.
  • Accessibility (macOS) — used only to paste at the cursor.
  • Screen Recording (macOS) — only if you enable screen context or system-audio meeting capture.

Questions: support@mantisware.co.za
