Questions

Is my voice private?

Yes. Local models run on your machine. Audio does not leave the device unless you choose a cloud provider and supply your own key.

Do I need the internet?

Not for local dictation after the model is downloaded. Updates, optional cloud APIs, and first-run model downloads need a connection.

Is it really free?

The app is MIT licensed. Local speech and local LLMs cost nothing. BYOK providers bill you directly if you use them.

What permissions does it need?

Microphone everywhere. Accessibility on macOS for auto-paste. Screen Recording on macOS only if you enable screen context or system-audio meeting capture.

Which Linux package should I use?

The official button is the AppImage. deb, rpm, and tar.gz are on the public share if you want them.

What are the system requirements?

8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB recommended for larger models. About 1 GB for the app, plus 75 MB–3 GB per speech model.
